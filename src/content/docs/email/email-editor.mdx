Each email step in a workflow is one email. You can edit it in two places, and both change the same email.

## Edit in the step's panel

Click an email step on the workflow canvas. The panel shows:

- **From**: your team, or the lead's owner
- **Subject** and **Preview text**, with **Insert data** for the person's details
- The email itself, as it will look

Click the email's words to type in place. Bold, italic, links and **Insert data** sit in the small bar above. **Rewrite with Tia** writes the email again in your brand's voice.

A footer with your address and an unsubscribe link is added for you when an email has none.

## Design on the canvas

Click **Open full editor** to open the email on the canvas. Every email in the workflow sits there side by side, and you can pan and zoom around them. Click **Back to workflow** when you're done. Your changes are already in the workflow.

<Steps>

1. **Pick a part**

   Click inside an email to pick a block, like a heading or a button. The **Element** and **Layout** panel on the right shows its settings: font, color, size, spacing, corners and more.

1. **Type in place**

   Double-click, or press Enter, to type in the picked part.

1. **Add blocks**

   Click **+** or press A to open the add panel. Add a heading, text, image, button, divider, spacer, columns, social links, a signature, a video and more. Drag blocks and sections within an email or between emails.

1. **Resize pictures**

   Drag a picture's corners to make it bigger or smaller.

1. **Copy and paste**

   ⌘C, ⌘X and ⌘V work across emails. One undo history covers the whole canvas.

</Steps>

### Tia on the canvas

Click **Ask Tia** at the top left. Tia's chat opens in a panel on the left. Pick a part and click **Add to chat** (or press ⌘L) to tell Tia what to change. Tia keeps her changes to what you added. You can also ask for several new emails in one prompt, like "Write a 3-email welcome series for new students", and they land side by side.

### Versions and comments

- **Versions**: each email keeps its earlier versions. Open the version menu above an email to preview one, then click **Restore version** to bring it back.
- **Comments**: press C or click **Comment** to leave a note on an email or on one part of it. Teammates can reply, and you can resolve a comment when it's done.

### Test it

Send a test to yourself from the email's menu. It goes through the real sending path, marked as a test.

## Publish the workflow

Edits to an email don't reach people until you publish its workflow. **Publish** shows every email once, with its picture, subject and sender and what changed. An email edited since the last publish shows **Changed since publish**.

## Email templates

**Email**, then **Templates**, holds emails you write once and reuse in workflows. Click **Create**, or use **Create**, then **Email template**, in the sidebar. Name it, design it on the canvas, and pick it later with **Use a template**.

## Your brand's voice

**Email**, then **Brand**, holds your brand book: your logo, colors, type, buttons and voice. Tia writes every email from the **Voice** brief, and you can change that brief by hand. See [Brand and domains](/workspace/brand-and-domains/).

<Aside type="note">
Emails send from Claritia's address until you set up your own domain. See [Your sending domain](/email/sending-domain/).
</Aside>
