October laid the ground for today's Claritia: forms that book meetings, and meetings that send their own emails. Parts built this month that have since been removed, like the CRM and website visitors, aren't listed.

## Week of October 27

### New

- **Forms and meetings work together.** A form's **On Submission** picks the meeting, and the meeting picks the person, with If and Then rules. **Dynamic meetings** route by answer.
- **Spam goes to Spam**, so spam fills never reach your tools.
- **Form results**: search, filters, columns, pages, bulk actions, export, and numbers for each question.
- **The forms engine** gains richer conditions, groups, lists that follow another answer, and calculated fields.
- **Booking pages** at `/booking/<handle>`, in Claritia's look, and **meeting webhooks**.
- **Workflows** get folders, and meeting reminders become workflows.
- **Forms**: alerts, auto-reply, spam rules, how long answers are kept, and the AI bar.
- **A new look** to match the website: Inter, black and white, with one green signal.
- **Faster screens** that answer on click.

### Fixed

- Leaving Forms no longer crashes the app.
- A form's page opens again after a reload.

## Week of October 20

### New

- **Meetings.** Meeting types, the slot engine, round robin, and booking that can't double book.
- **Calendars.** Google Calendar, Outlook and Zoom connect in one click. Busy times stay fresh with pushes and a 10-minute check, and events go out without duplicate invites.
- **Public booking.** Booking pages, embeds, the guest's manage link, one-off and single-use links, polls, and paid meeting types on Stripe.
- **Group meetings**: every seat shares one event and video link.
- **Meeting emails**: confirmations with calendar invites, reminders, updates, cancels, an after-meeting check, no-show follow-up and "book later". Poll voters get every email once. Hard bounces and complaints stop emails to that address.
- **A host gets an email** when their calendar needs reconnecting.
- **Book a meeting after a form**, tied back to the fill, with the calendar shown only to the leads that match.
- **Forms**: the AI chat builder, your brand read from your website, a daily check of every live form, and delivery to CRMs, Slack and webhooks.
- **Send leads to Pipedrive, Attio and Close.**
- **The command bar** and a new sign-in.

## Week of October 13

### New

- **Sign-in and workspaces.**
- **API keys and the REST API.**
- **Sending email from your own domain**: verified domains, a check at send time, and one-click unsubscribe.
- **Forms**: the first builder, embed, UTM capture, spam checks and consent.
- **Settings** as a grouped side list of pages.
- **Billing** with Stripe.
