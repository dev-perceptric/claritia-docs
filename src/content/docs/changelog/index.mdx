Claritia ships changes most days. These notes list what you'd notice in the app, month by month, newest first. Behind-the-scenes work, like speed-ups you can't see and server moves, is left out.

## Latest

- **November 18, 2026**: getting-started emails, with ten tips in a new workspace's first weeks. Turn them off in **Settings**, then **Profile**.
- **November 17, 2026**: a new sign-up with a code, a bot check and a guided setup.
- **November 16, 2026**: a new **Settings › Integrations** page, with app-store cards, search, one category menu and a detail sheet for every tool.
- **November 14, 2026**: Meeting settings is gone. Stripe moved to **Integrations**, and meeting webhooks to **Settings › Webhooks**.
- **November 13, 2026**: booking page round 2, with one-click **Confirm**, 5-minute holds, a week view and **Show my calendar**.
