HubSpot is a CRM, a place where a business keeps track of every customer and lead. Teams use it to store contacts and companies, send marketing emails, and follow deals until they close. It has a free plan, so many small teams start there.

With Claritia, you can send each form lead to HubSpot as a contact.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | CRMs |
| Connects with | Sign in with HubSpot |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect HubSpot

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "HubSpot" in **Search integrations**, or pick **CRMs** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while HubSpot's sign-in page loads. Sign in to HubSpot and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

If HubSpot's sign-in isn't set up on Claritia's side, Connect tells you and opens a short form for a **Private app token** instead. You can also open the card and click **Use a key instead**. Keys are only a fallback.

## What Claritia can do in HubSpot

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Create new contacts in HubSpot.
- Update contacts that are already in HubSpot, found by their email.
- See the people on your HubSpot team, so a new lead can get the right owner.
- See your HubSpot lists and add contacts to them.
- Add a few contact fields that Claritia fills in.

## Send a form's leads to HubSpot

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form and click **Sync**. The left side lists each connected tool with its state, such as **Not sending leads** or **Synced 48 this month**. Pick **HubSpot**.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send leads to HubSpot**. Claritia says **Leads go to HubSpot now**.

1. **Choose what happens to people already there**

   Under **When the email is already in HubSpot**, pick **Update it** or **Leave it**. **Make a new one** is greyed out, because HubSpot keeps one contact per email. Claritia matches people by email.

1. **Set the owner**

   **Owner** is **Routing's pick**, the person your form's routing or meeting assigned, or **HubSpot decides**, so HubSpot's own assignment rules pick. With Routing's pick, records HubSpot already owns keep their owner.

1. **Send consent**

   Turn on **Send email consent** to pass on who ticked your form's consent box, as HubSpot's subscription status.

1. **Check the fields**

   The **Fields** table lists each question and the **HubSpot field** it fills, or **Not sent**. Change the field on each question in **Build**. A problem shows **Fix**, and a choice question whose answers don't match HubSpot's options shows **Check choices**. Fields that match show **OK**.

</Steps>

### Leads that didn't arrive

If a lead didn't reach HubSpot, a red line at the top of the pane says how many didn't arrive this week and why. Click **Send again** to retry them. When HubSpot turned a lead down as a duplicate, **Create anyway** makes it.

### Stop sending

At the bottom of the pane, **Stop sending to HubSpot** has **Turn off**. Leads keep going to Claritia, and you can click **Turn on** any time.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to HubSpot. Claritia also reads your HubSpot users, so a lead can get an owner, and your fields, so questions can fill them. |
| What's sent | Each fill's contact: name, email, phone, job title, company, and the answers you map to fields. The owner and consent if you turn them on. |
| Match | By email. One person stays one record. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every demo request from your website shows up in HubSpot as a new contact, with name, email, phone and job title.
- Someone who fills two forms stays one contact in HubSpot, updated with their latest answers.
- New leads go straight onto the HubSpot list you choose, so your follow-up emails start on their own.
- The salesperson Claritia picks for a lead becomes the contact owner in HubSpot.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the HubSpot card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect HubSpot?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in HubSpot.

<Aside type="tip">
HubSpot's free plan works. Pick a HubSpot list in the form's Sync tab and new leads join it, so HubSpot's own follow-ups can start.
</Aside>
