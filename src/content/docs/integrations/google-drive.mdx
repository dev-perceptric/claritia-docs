Google Drive is Google's cloud storage for files and folders. Teams keep their documents there and share them with each other and with clients.

With Claritia, you can copy uploaded files to Google Drive.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Documents and files |
| Connects with | Sign in with Google Drive |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect Google Drive

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Google Drive" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Documents and files** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Google Drive's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Google Drive and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Google Drive

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Create folders inside the folder you pick.
- Upload the files people send through your forms.
- Only work inside that folder.

## Copy uploaded files to Google Drive

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Google Drive** on the left. Your form needs a file upload question.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Copy files to Google Drive**, or switch on **Copy each fill's files**.

1. **Pick the folder**

   Choose from **Pick a folder**. Claritia says **Files go to [folder] now**.

</Steps>

Each fill's files are copied into that folder in Google Drive. To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop copying files to Google Drive?**.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Google Drive. |
| What's sent | The files people upload in the form, copied into the folder you pick. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Files people upload in your forms are saved to Google Drive automatically.
- Each fill gets its own folder, named so you can find it.
- Resumes, photos and documents end up where your team already looks.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Google Drive card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Google Drive?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Google Drive.
