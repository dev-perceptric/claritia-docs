Freshsales is the CRM from Freshworks. It has phone, email and lead scoring built in, so sales teams can call and email leads without switching tools.

With Claritia, you can send each form lead to Freshsales as a contact.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | CRMs |
| Connects with | A key from your Freshsales account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect Freshsales

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Freshsales" in **Search integrations**, or pick **CRMs** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   Freshsales connects with a key from your Freshsales account. Connect opens a secure connect page that asks for it. If that page isn't set up, the card's sheet opens a short form instead, with:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **Freshsales address** | Yes | `acme.myfreshworks.com` |
   | **API key** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with Freshsales. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what Freshsales said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Freshsales

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Create new contacts in Freshsales.
- Update contacts that are already in Freshsales, found by their email.
- See the people on your Freshsales team, so a new lead can get the right owner.

## Send a form's leads to Freshsales

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form and click **Sync**. The left side lists each connected tool with its state, such as **Not sending leads** or **Synced 48 this month**. Pick **Freshsales**.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send leads to Freshsales**. Claritia says **Leads go to Freshsales now**.

1. **Choose what happens to people already there**

   Under **When the email is already in Freshsales**, pick **Update it** or **Leave it**. **Make a new one** is greyed out, because Freshsales keeps one contact per email. Claritia matches people by email.

1. **Set the owner**

   **Owner** is **Routing's pick**, the person your form's routing or meeting assigned, or **Freshsales decides**, so Freshsales's own assignment rules pick. With Routing's pick, records Freshsales already owns keep their owner.

1. **Send consent**

   Turn on **Send email consent** to pass on who ticked your form's consent box, as Freshsales's subscription status.

1. **Check the fields**

   The **Fields** table lists each question and the **Freshsales field** it fills, or **Not sent**. Change the field on each question in **Build**. A problem shows **Fix**, and a choice question whose answers don't match Freshsales's options shows **Check choices**. Fields that match show **OK**.

</Steps>

### Leads that didn't arrive

If a lead didn't reach Freshsales, a red line at the top of the pane says how many didn't arrive this week and why. Click **Send again** to retry them. When Freshsales turned a lead down as a duplicate, **Create anyway** makes it.

### Stop sending

At the bottom of the pane, **Stop sending to Freshsales** has **Turn off**. Leads keep going to Claritia, and you can click **Turn on** any time.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Freshsales. Claritia also reads your Freshsales users, so a lead can get an owner, and your fields, so questions can fill them. |
| What's sent | Each fill's contact: name, email, phone, job title, company, and the answers you map to fields. The owner and consent if you turn them on. |
| Match | By email. One person stays one record. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every form fill becomes a contact in Freshsales.
- A returning person gets updated, not duplicated.
- Your reps see new leads in Freshsales right away.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Freshsales card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Freshsales?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Freshsales.
