Discord is a chat app for communities and teams. Groups talk in channels by text, voice and video, and many online communities live there.

With Claritia, you can post form alerts in Discord.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Messages and texts |
| Connects with | Sign in with Discord |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab and workflow steps |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect Discord

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Discord" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Messages and texts** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Discord's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Discord and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

If Discord's sign-in isn't set up on Claritia's side, Connect tells you and opens a short form for a **Channel webhook address** instead. You can also open the card and click **Use a key instead**. Keys are only a fallback.

Discord can sign in, or take a **Channel webhook address**, like `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/123/abc`. Alerts post to that channel.

## What Claritia can do in Discord

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Post messages in the channel you link.

## Post an alert for each fill

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Discord** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Alert Discord on each fill**, or switch on **Alert on each fill**.

1. **The channel is set**

   Alerts post to the channel whose webhook address you pasted when you connected. Claritia says **Alerts go to Discord now**.

</Steps>

To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop alerts in Discord?**.

### Post from a workflow

In a workflow, add the **Teams or Discord** step, in the **Outside** group. Pick Discord and write the message. It starts as "A new meeting was just booked."

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Discord. |
| What's sent | A message for each fill, and any message a workflow's **Teams or Discord** step sends. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Get a Discord message every time someone fills a form.
- Your community team sees new sign-ups as they happen.
- Workflows can post updates to your server.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Discord card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Discord?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Discord.
