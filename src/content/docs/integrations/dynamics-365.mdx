Microsoft Dynamics 365 is Microsoft's CRM for sales and customer service. It's common in larger companies that already run on Microsoft.

With Claritia, you can send each form lead to Dynamics 365 as a contact.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | CRMs |
| Connects with | Sign in with Dynamics 365 |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Dynamics 365

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Dynamics 365" in **Search integrations**, or pick **CRMs** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Dynamics 365's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Dynamics 365 and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Dynamics 365

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Create new contacts in Dynamics 365.
- Update contacts that are already in Dynamics 365, found by their email.
- See the people on your Dynamics 365 team, so a new lead can get the right owner.

## Send a form's leads to Dynamics 365

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form and click **Sync**. The left side lists each connected tool with its state, such as **Not sending leads** or **Synced 48 this month**. Pick **Dynamics 365**.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send leads to Dynamics 365**. Claritia says **Leads go to Dynamics 365 now**.

1. **Choose what happens to people already there**

   Under **When the email is already in Dynamics 365**, pick **Update it** or **Leave it**. **Make a new one** is greyed out, because Dynamics 365 keeps one contact per email. Claritia matches people by email.

1. **Set the owner**

   **Owner** is **Routing's pick**, the person your form's routing or meeting assigned, or **Dynamics 365 decides**, so Dynamics 365's own assignment rules pick. With Routing's pick, records Dynamics 365 already owns keep their owner.

1. **Send consent**

   Turn on **Send email consent** to pass on who ticked your form's consent box, as Dynamics 365's subscription status.

1. **Check the fields**

   The **Fields** table lists each question and the **Dynamics 365 field** it fills, or **Not sent**. Change the field on each question in **Build**. A problem shows **Fix**, and a choice question whose answers don't match Dynamics 365's options shows **Check choices**. Fields that match show **OK**.

</Steps>

### Leads that didn't arrive

If a lead didn't reach Dynamics 365, a red line at the top of the pane says how many didn't arrive this week and why. Click **Send again** to retry them. When Dynamics 365 turned a lead down as a duplicate, **Create anyway** makes it.

### Stop sending

At the bottom of the pane, **Stop sending to Dynamics 365** has **Turn off**. Leads keep going to Claritia, and you can click **Turn on** any time.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Dynamics 365. Claritia also reads your Dynamics 365 users, so a lead can get an owner, and your fields, so questions can fill them. |
| What's sent | Each fill's contact: name, email, phone, job title, company, and the answers you map to fields. The owner and consent if you turn them on. |
| Match | By email. One person stays one record. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every form fill becomes a contact in Dynamics 365.
- A returning person updates their existing contact.
- Your Microsoft-based sales team sees new leads without leaving Dynamics.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Dynamics 365 card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Dynamics 365?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Dynamics 365.
