Attio is a modern CRM that you shape around your own data. It's popular with startups that want something more flexible than a classic CRM.

With Claritia, you can send each form lead to Attio as a contact.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | CRMs |
| Connects with | Sign in with Attio |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Attio

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Attio" in **Search integrations**, or pick **CRMs** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Attio's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Attio and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

If Attio's sign-in isn't set up on Claritia's side, Connect tells you and opens a short form for an **Access token** instead. You can also open the card and click **Use a key instead**. Keys are only a fallback.

## What Claritia can do in Attio

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Create new contacts in Attio.
- Update contacts that are already in Attio, found by their email.
- See the people on your Attio team, so a new lead can get the right owner.

## Send a form's leads to Attio

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form and click **Sync**. The left side lists each connected tool with its state, such as **Not sending leads** or **Synced 48 this month**. Pick **Attio**.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send leads to Attio**. Claritia says **Leads go to Attio now**.

1. **Choose what happens to people already there**

   Under **When the email is already in Attio**, pick **Update it** or **Leave it**. **Make a new one** is greyed out, because Attio keeps one contact per email. Claritia matches people by email.

1. **Set the owner**

   **Owner** is **Routing's pick**, the person your form's routing or meeting assigned, or **Attio decides**, so Attio's own assignment rules pick. With Routing's pick, records Attio already owns keep their owner.

1. **Send consent**

   Turn on **Send email consent** to pass on who ticked your form's consent box, as Attio's subscription status.

1. **Check the fields**

   The **Fields** table lists each question and the **Attio field** it fills, or **Not sent**. Change the field on each question in **Build**. A problem shows **Fix**, and a choice question whose answers don't match Attio's options shows **Check choices**. Fields that match show **OK**.

</Steps>

### Leads that didn't arrive

If a lead didn't reach Attio, a red line at the top of the pane says how many didn't arrive this week and why. Click **Send again** to retry them. When Attio turned a lead down as a duplicate, **Create anyway** makes it.

### Stop sending

At the bottom of the pane, **Stop sending to Attio** has **Turn off**. Leads keep going to Claritia, and you can click **Turn on** any time.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Attio. Claritia also reads your Attio users, so a lead can get an owner, and your fields, so questions can fill them. |
| What's sent | Each fill's contact: name, email, phone, job title, company, and the answers you map to fields. The owner and consent if you turn them on. |
| Match | By email. One person stays one record. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every form fill becomes a person record in Attio.
- A returning person updates their existing record.
- Custom questions fill the Attio attributes you link them to.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Attio card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Attio?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Attio.
