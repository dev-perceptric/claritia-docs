PostHog is a product analytics tool. Teams use it to see what people do in their app, watch session replays and turn features on and off.

With Claritia, you can send form fills to PostHog as events.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Analytics and ads |
| Connects with | A key from your PostHog account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab and workflow steps |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect PostHog

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "PostHog" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Analytics and ads** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   PostHog has no sign-in for apps like Claritia, so the card's sheet opens a short form that asks for:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **Project API key** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |
   | **PostHog address** | No | `https://us.i.posthog.com` |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with PostHog. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what PostHog said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in PostHog

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Send events to PostHog, like a form fill or a booked meeting.

## Send fills to PostHog

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **PostHog** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send fills to PostHog**, or switch on **Form Submitted event on each fill**. Claritia says **Fills go to PostHog now**.

</Steps>

Each fill sends one **Form Submitted** event. To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop sending fills to PostHog?**.

### Send an event from a workflow

In a workflow, add the **Analytics event** step, in the **Outside** group. Under **Pick a tool**, choose PostHog, and type an **Event name**, like `Meeting Booked`. Until a tool is connected, the step says **Connect Segment, PostHog, Mixpanel or Amplitude first**.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to PostHog. |
| What's sent | A **Form Submitted** event for each fill, and any event a workflow's **Analytics event** step sends. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- See form fills and bookings in PostHog next to everything else people do.
- Measure which pages and campaigns bring in leads.
- Workflows can send their own events, like a lead becoming qualified.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the PostHog card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect PostHog?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in PostHog.
