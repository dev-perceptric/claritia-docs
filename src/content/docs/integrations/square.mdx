Square takes payments online and in person. Many shops, cafes and service businesses already use it for their card readers and online sales.

With Claritia, you can take Square payments for paid meetings.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Payments |
| Connects with | A key from your Square account |
| Used in | A meeting type's **Payment** |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Square

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Square" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Payments** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   Square has no sign-in for apps like Claritia, so the card's sheet opens a short form that asks for:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **Access token** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |
   | **Location ID** | Yes |  |
   | **Webhook signature key** | No |  Hidden as you type. |
   | **Environment** | No | `production or sandbox` |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with Square. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what Square said.

</Steps>

Set **Environment** to `sandbox` to test, or leave it for `production`. The **Webhook signature key** lets Claritia confirm payments when a guest never comes back to the page.

## What Claritia can do in Square

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Take payments for your paid meetings, paid into your Square account.

## Take Square payments for meetings

<Steps>

1. **Open a meeting type**

   Go to **Meeting types** and click the card, then **All settings**.

1. **Turn on payment**

   Under **Payment**, turn on **Take payment** and set the **Amount** and **Currency**.

1. **Pick Square**

   Set **Paid with** to **Square**. Click **Save changes**.

</Steps>

When a guest books, Claritia holds the time for 10 minutes and sends them to Square to pay. The meeting shows **Waiting on payment** until Claritia reads the payment back from Square. Then it's booked, and the invite and emails go out. If someone else took the time meanwhile, the payment is refunded at once. If checkout can't start, the meeting is canceled and the time freed.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Square to start checkout, then Square to Claritia to confirm the payment. |
| What's sent | The meeting's name, the amount, the currency and the guest's email. |
| When | At each booking. |

## What teams use it for

- Charge for bookings with the same account you use in your shop.
- Money goes straight to your Square account.
- Guests pay by card before the meeting is confirmed.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Square card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Square?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Square.
