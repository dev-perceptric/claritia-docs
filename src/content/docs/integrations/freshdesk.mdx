Freshdesk is the help desk from Freshworks. Support teams answer customer questions as tickets.

With Claritia, you can make a Freshdesk ticket for each form fill.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Tasks and tickets |
| Connects with | A key from your Freshdesk account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Freshdesk

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Freshdesk" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Tasks and tickets** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   Freshdesk connects with a key from your Freshdesk account. Connect opens a secure connect page that asks for it. If that page isn't set up, the card's sheet opens a short form instead, with:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **Freshdesk address** | Yes | `acme.freshdesk.com` |
   | **API key** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with Freshdesk. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what Freshdesk said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Freshdesk

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your Freshdesk help desk.
- Create a new ticket for every form fill.
- Find tickets it made before, so it never makes the same one twice.

## Make a Freshdesk ticket for each fill

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Freshdesk** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send rows to Freshdesk**.

1. **Pick the group**

   Choose from **Pick a group**. It reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads them from Freshdesk. Claritia says **Fills go to [name] now**.

</Steps>

Each fill becomes one ticket in that group, with every answer inside.

To switch, use **Use another table** or pick again. To stop, click **Turn off** and confirm. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Freshdesk. Claritia reads your groups so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | One ticket for each fill, with every answer. |
| Repeats | A fill sent twice still makes only one. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Support forms become Freshdesk tickets.
- Every answer from the form is inside the ticket.
- Your support team works from one inbox.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Freshdesk card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Freshdesk?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Freshdesk.
