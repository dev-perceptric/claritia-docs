Segment collects data about what your customers do and sends it to your other tools. Teams use it so every tool sees the same customer data.

With Claritia, you can send form fills to Segment as events.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Analytics and ads |
| Connects with | A key from your Segment account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab and workflow steps |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Segment

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Segment" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Analytics and ads** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   Segment has no sign-in for apps like Claritia, so the card's sheet opens a short form that asks for:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **Write key** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |
   | **Region (us or eu)** | No | `us` |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with Segment. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what Segment said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Segment

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Send events to Segment, like a form fill or a booked meeting.

## Send fills to Segment

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Segment** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send fills to Segment**, or switch on **Form Submitted event on each fill**. Claritia says **Fills go to Segment now**.

</Steps>

Each fill sends one **Form Submitted** event. To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop sending fills to Segment?**.

### Send an event from a workflow

In a workflow, add the **Analytics event** step, in the **Outside** group. Under **Pick a tool**, choose Segment, and type an **Event name**, like `Meeting Booked`. Until a tool is connected, the step says **Connect Segment, PostHog, Mixpanel or Amplitude first**.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Segment. |
| What's sent | A **Form Submitted** event for each fill, and any event a workflow's **Analytics event** step sends. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- See form fills and bookings in Segment next to everything else people do.
- Measure which pages and campaigns bring in leads.
- Workflows can send their own events, like a lead becoming qualified.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Segment card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Segment?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Segment.
