Salesforce is the CRM that most large sales teams use. It holds leads, contacts, companies and deals, and companies build their whole sales process around it.

With Claritia, you can send each form lead to Salesforce as a contact.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | CRMs |
| Connects with | Sign in with Salesforce |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Business |

## Connect Salesforce

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Salesforce" in **Search integrations**, or pick **CRMs** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Salesforce's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Salesforce and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Salesforce

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Create new contacts in Salesforce.
- Update contacts that are already in Salesforce, found by their email.
- See the people on your Salesforce team, so a new lead can get the right owner.

## Send a form's leads to Salesforce

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form and click **Sync**. The left side lists each connected tool with its state, such as **Not sending leads** or **Synced 48 this month**. Pick **Salesforce**.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send leads to Salesforce**. Claritia says **Leads go to Salesforce now**.

1. **Pick what to make**

   Set **Make them as** to **Contacts** or **Leads**. Leads is the default for a new destination.

1. **Choose what happens to people already there**

   Under **When the email is already in Salesforce**, pick **Update it** or **Leave it**. **Make a new one** is greyed out, because Salesforce keeps one contact per email. Claritia matches people by email.

1. **Set the owner**

   **Owner** is **Routing's pick**, the person your form's routing or meeting assigned, or **Salesforce decides**, so Salesforce's own assignment rules pick. With Routing's pick, records Salesforce already owns keep their owner.

1. **Send consent**

   Turn on **Send email consent** to pass on who ticked your form's consent box, as Salesforce's subscription status.

1. **Check the fields**

   The **Fields** table lists each question and the **Salesforce field** it fills, or **Not sent**. Change the field on each question in **Build**. A problem shows **Fix**, and a choice question whose answers don't match Salesforce's options shows **Check choices**. Fields that match show **OK**.

</Steps>

### Leads that didn't arrive

If a lead didn't reach Salesforce, a red line at the top of the pane says how many didn't arrive this week and why. Click **Send again** to retry them. When Salesforce turned a lead down as a duplicate, **Create anyway** makes it.

### Stop sending

At the bottom of the pane, **Stop sending to Salesforce** has **Turn off**. Leads keep going to Claritia, and you can click **Turn on** any time.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Salesforce. Claritia also reads your Salesforce users, so a lead can get an owner, and your fields, so questions can fill them. |
| What's sent | Each fill's contact: name, email, phone, job title, company, and the answers you map to fields. The owner and consent if you turn them on. |
| Match | By email. One person stays one record. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every form fill becomes a lead or contact in Salesforce, ready for your sales team.
- A person who already exists in Salesforce gets updated instead of duplicated.
- The rep Claritia routes a lead to becomes its owner in Salesforce.
- Your answers to custom questions go into the Salesforce fields you link them to.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Salesforce card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Salesforce?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Salesforce.

<Aside type="note">
Salesforce is on the Business plan. On a lower plan, the card shows **Business** and **Upgrade**, and new fills skip Salesforce with **Salesforce is on Business.** Pick **Leads** or **Contacts** per form under **Make them as**.
</Aside>
