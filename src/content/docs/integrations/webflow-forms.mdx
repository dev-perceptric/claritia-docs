Webflow is a website builder for designers. It lets teams design and launch websites without code, and every site can have its own forms.

With Claritia, you can bring Webflow forms answers into Claritia.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Forms |
| Connects with | Webhook address you paste in the tool |
| Used in | Settings › Integrations |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Webflow forms

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Webflow forms" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Forms** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   Webflow forms doesn't sign its webhooks, so there's nothing to type. Claritia makes your webhook address at once and shows it on the card with **Copy**.

1. **Copy the webhook address**

   The address is shown under the card with a **Copy** button. Later, click **Webhook URL** on the card to see it again.

1. **Paste it in Webflow forms**

   Add a webhook in Webflow forms with that address. See the steps below.

</Steps>

### Add the webhook in Webflow

The steps in Webflow's own screens may change (check).

<Steps>

1. **Open the site's settings**

   In Webflow, open **Site settings**, then **Apps & Integrations**, then **Webhooks**, and click **Add webhook**.

1. **Pick the trigger**

   Set the trigger type to **Form submission** and paste Claritia's address as the URL.

1. **Publish the site**

   Publish so the form is live, then send the form once on the live site to check.

</Steps>

Webflow has no signing secret for form webhooks. Claritia checks the token in the address instead, so keep the address private.

## What Claritia can do in Webflow forms

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Claritia only receives the answers Webflow sends to it.
- Claritia can't see or change anything else in your Webflow account.

## What happens to each fill

Each fill in Webflow forms is sent to Claritia the moment it's made. Claritia matches the person by email, so a person who fills two forms stays one person. Answers come in by their question titles. Claritia never reads anything from Webflow forms on its own.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Webflow forms to Claritia, by webhook. |
| What's sent | Each fill's answers, by question title, and any hidden fields. |
| When | Within seconds of each fill or booking. Claritia never polls. |

## What teams use it for

- Keep using Webflow forms and still get every answer into Claritia.
- Send Webflow leads to your CRM, email lists and workflows through Claritia.
- Book meetings for people who fill your Webflow forms.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Webflow forms card and confirm **Disconnect Webflow forms?**. The webhook address stops working, so also remove the webhook in Webflow forms.
