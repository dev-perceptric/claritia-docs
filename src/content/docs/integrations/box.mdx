Box is cloud storage and file sharing made for businesses. Larger companies use it because of its security controls and its record of who opened what.

With Claritia, you can copy uploaded files to Box.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Documents and files |
| Connects with | Sign in with Box |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Box

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Box" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Documents and files** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Box's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Box and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Box

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Create folders inside the folder you pick.
- Upload the files people send through your forms.
- Only work inside that folder.

## Copy uploaded files to Box

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Box** on the left. Your form needs a file upload question.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Copy files to Box**, or switch on **Copy each fill's files**.

1. **Pick the folder**

   Choose from **Pick a folder**. Claritia says **Files go to [folder] now**.

</Steps>

Each fill's files are copied into that folder in Box. To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop copying files to Box?**.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Box. |
| What's sent | The files people upload in the form, copied into the folder you pick. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Files people upload in your forms are saved to Box automatically.
- Each fill gets its own folder, named so you can find it.
- Resumes, photos and documents end up where your team already looks.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Box card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Box?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Box.
