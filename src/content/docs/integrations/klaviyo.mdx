Klaviyo is an email and text marketing tool built for online stores. Shops use it to send product emails based on what customers do.

With Claritia, you can add form sign-ups to a Klaviyo list.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Email lists |
| Connects with | A key from your Klaviyo account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect Klaviyo

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Klaviyo" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Email lists** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   Klaviyo connects with a key from your Klaviyo account. Connect opens a secure connect page that asks for it. If that page isn't set up, the card's sheet opens a short form instead, with:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **Private API key** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with Klaviyo. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what Klaviyo said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Klaviyo

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your Klaviyo lists.
- Add new subscribers to the list you choose.
- Update subscribers who are already on that list.

## Add a form's sign-ups to a Klaviyo list

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Klaviyo** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Add leads to Klaviyo**.

1. **Pick the list**

   Choose from **Pick a list**. The menu reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads your lists from Klaviyo. Claritia confirms with **Leads join [name] now**.

1. **Choose who subscribes**

   Set **Subscribe** to **Everyone** or **Who ticked consent**.

</Steps>

To stop, click **Turn off** under **Stop sending to Klaviyo** and confirm **Stop adding leads to Klaviyo?**. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Klaviyo. Claritia reads your lists so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | The person's email and name, and their consent. |
| Match | By email. People already on the list are updated. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Form sign-ups join the Klaviyo list you pick, so your welcome emails start.
- Quiz answers go with the subscriber, so you can send the right product emails.
- A returning subscriber gets updated, not added twice.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Klaviyo card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Klaviyo?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Klaviyo.
