PandaDoc creates proposals, quotes and contracts that people can sign online. Sales teams use its templates to send good-looking documents in minutes.

With Claritia, you can make a PandaDoc document for each form fill.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Documents and files |
| Connects with | A key from your PandaDoc account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect PandaDoc

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "PandaDoc" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Documents and files** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   PandaDoc connects with a key from your PandaDoc account. Connect opens a secure connect page that asks for it. If that page isn't set up, the card's sheet opens a short form instead, with:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **API key** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with PandaDoc. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what PandaDoc said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in PandaDoc

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your PandaDoc templates.
- Create and send a document from a template.
- Fill it with the person's answers.

## Make a document for each fill

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **PandaDoc** on the left.

1. **Turn on A document for each fill**

   Switch on **A document for each fill**.

1. **Pick the template**

   Choose from **Pick a template**. It reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads your PandaDoc templates. Claritia says **Each fill gets [template] now**.

</Steps>

Answers drop into the template in the places you marked. To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop making documents in PandaDoc?**.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to PandaDoc. Claritia reads the template you pick. |
| What's sent | A new document for each fill, made from your template, with the answers filled in. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- A filled form turns into a proposal or quote, sent to sign.
- Prices and details come from the person's answers.
- Your sales team stops building documents by hand.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the PandaDoc card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect PandaDoc?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in PandaDoc.
