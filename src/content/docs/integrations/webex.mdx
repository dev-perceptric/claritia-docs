Webex is Cisco's video meeting app. Many large companies use it for calls, webinars and team meetings.

With Claritia, you can give each booked meeting a Webex link.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Meetings |
| Connects with | Sign in with Webex |
| Used in | A meeting type's **Location** |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Webex

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Webex" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Meetings** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Webex's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Webex and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Webex

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Create Webex meetings for the calls people book with you.

## Use Webex for meetings

<Steps>

1. **Open a meeting type**

   Go to **Meeting types** and click the card.

1. **Pick Webex as the location**

   In **Location**, pick **Webex**. Click **Save changes**.

</Steps>

Each booking gets its own Webex meeting. It's made on your team's own Webex account, moves with the booking and is taken down when the meeting is canceled. The link goes in the calendar invite and the confirmation email, and in the meeting's webhook as `location.url`.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Webex. |
| What's sent | A new Webex meeting for each booking. |
| When | At each booking. |

## What teams use it for

- Every meeting booked through Claritia gets its own Webex link.
- Guests get the link in their confirmation email.
- Your team keeps using Webex for calls.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Webex card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Webex?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Webex.
