Mailchimp is an email marketing tool for newsletters and campaigns. It's one of the most used email tools for small businesses.

With Claritia, you can add form sign-ups to a Mailchimp list.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Email lists |
| Connects with | Sign in with Mailchimp |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect Mailchimp

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Mailchimp" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Email lists** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Mailchimp's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Mailchimp and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

If Mailchimp's sign-in isn't set up on Claritia's side, Connect tells you and opens a short form for an **API key** instead. You can also open the card and click **Use a key instead**. Keys are only a fallback.

## What Claritia can do in Mailchimp

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your Mailchimp lists.
- Add new subscribers to the list you choose.
- Update subscribers who are already on that list.

## Add a form's sign-ups to a Mailchimp audience

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Mailchimp** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Add leads to Mailchimp**.

1. **Pick the audience**

   Choose from **Pick a audience**. The menu reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads your audiences from Mailchimp. Claritia confirms with **Leads join [name] now**.

1. **Choose who subscribes**

   Set **Subscribe** to **Everyone** or **Who ticked consent**.

1. **Add tags**

   Under **Tags**, type a tag in **Add a tag** and press Enter. Each new person gets those tags in Mailchimp. Click a tag's x to remove it.

</Steps>

To stop, click **Turn off** under **Stop sending to Mailchimp** and confirm **Stop adding leads to Mailchimp?**. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Mailchimp. Claritia reads your audiences so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | The person's email and name, their consent, and your tags. |
| Match | By email. People already on the audience are updated. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- People who sign up through your form join your Mailchimp audience.
- Webinar sign-ups go onto their own list, so you can send reminders from Mailchimp.
- Someone already on your list gets their details updated, not added twice.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Mailchimp card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Mailchimp?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Mailchimp.
