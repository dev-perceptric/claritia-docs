Zoom is a video meeting app. Many teams use it for sales calls, demos and client meetings.

With Claritia, each meeting booked with you gets its own Zoom meeting, made on your Zoom account. The link goes in the calendar invite and the confirmation email.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Video meetings |
| Connects with | Sign in with Zoom |
| Used in | **Meetings**, then **Availability**, then **Calendars**, and a meeting type's **Location** |
| Who can connect | Each person connects their own Zoom |
| Plan | Every plan |

## Connect Zoom

<Steps>

1. **Open Availability**

   In the sidebar, under **Meetings**, click **Availability**, then the **Calendars** tab.

1. **Click Zoom**

   Under your calendar accounts, click **Zoom**. Sign in to Zoom and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   Zoom shows in your list of accounts with its email. Zoom has no calendars to pick, so there's nothing else to set.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Zoom

- Make a Zoom meeting for each booking with you (`meeting:write`).

Claritia doesn't read your Zoom meetings or recordings.

## Give a meeting type a Zoom link

<Steps>

1. **Open the meeting type**

   Go to **Meeting types** and click the card.

1. **Pick Zoom as the location**

   In **Location**, pick **Zoom**. Click **Save changes**.

</Steps>

Each booking now gets its own Zoom link. On a team meeting, each host who takes Zoom meetings connects their own Zoom.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Zoom. |
| What's sent | The meeting's name and time, to make one Zoom meeting per booking. |
| When | Right after each booking, with the calendar event. Moving or canceling the meeting changes the Zoom meeting too. |

## Disconnect

In **Calendars**, open the Zoom account's menu and click **Disconnect**. Confirm **Disconnect [email]?**. New bookings of Zoom meeting types can't get a Zoom link until you connect again, so pick another **Location** first.
