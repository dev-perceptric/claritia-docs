Brevo is an email, text and chat marketing tool. It used to be called Sendinblue. Small businesses use it for newsletters, promotions and automatic welcome emails.

With Claritia, you can add form sign-ups to a Brevo list.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Email lists |
| Connects with | A key from your Brevo account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Brevo

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Brevo" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Email lists** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   Brevo connects with a key from your Brevo account. Connect opens a secure connect page that asks for it. If that page isn't set up, the card's sheet opens a short form instead, with:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **API key** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with Brevo. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what Brevo said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Brevo

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your Brevo lists.
- Add new subscribers to the list you choose.
- Update subscribers who are already on that list.

## Add a form's sign-ups to a Brevo list

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Brevo** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Add leads to Brevo**.

1. **Pick the list**

   Choose from **Pick a list**. The menu reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads your lists from Brevo. Claritia confirms with **Leads join [name] now**.

1. **Choose who subscribes**

   Set **Subscribe** to **Everyone** or **Who ticked consent**.

</Steps>

To stop, click **Turn off** under **Stop sending to Brevo** and confirm **Stop adding leads to Brevo?**. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Brevo. Claritia reads your lists so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | The person's email and name, and their consent. |
| Match | By email. People already on the list are updated. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Form sign-ups join the Brevo list you choose.
- A returning contact gets updated, not duplicated.
- Your Brevo campaigns reach new leads right away.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Brevo card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Brevo?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Brevo.
