ActiveCampaign is an email marketing and automation tool. Teams use it to send email sequences that react to what people do.

With Claritia, you can add form sign-ups to a ActiveCampaign list.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Email lists |
| Connects with | A key from your ActiveCampaign account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect ActiveCampaign

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "ActiveCampaign" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Email lists** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   ActiveCampaign connects with a key from your ActiveCampaign account. Connect opens a secure connect page that asks for it. If that page isn't set up, the card's sheet opens a short form instead, with:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **API URL** | Yes | `https://you.api-us1.com` |
   | **API key** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with ActiveCampaign. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what ActiveCampaign said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in ActiveCampaign

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your ActiveCampaign lists.
- Add new subscribers to the list you choose.
- Update subscribers who are already on that list.

## Add a form's sign-ups to a ActiveCampaign list

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **ActiveCampaign** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Add leads to ActiveCampaign**.

1. **Pick the list**

   Choose from **Pick a list**. The menu reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads your lists from ActiveCampaign. Claritia confirms with **Leads join [name] now**.

1. **Choose who subscribes**

   Set **Subscribe** to **Everyone** or **Who ticked consent**.

1. **Add tags**

   Under **Tags**, type a tag in **Add a tag** and press Enter. Each new person gets those tags in ActiveCampaign. Click a tag's x to remove it.

</Steps>

To stop, click **Turn off** under **Stop sending to ActiveCampaign** and confirm **Stop adding leads to ActiveCampaign?**. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to ActiveCampaign. Claritia reads your lists so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | The person's email and name, their consent, and your tags. |
| Match | By email. People already on the list are updated. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Form sign-ups join your ActiveCampaign list and start your automations.
- A returning contact gets updated with their newest answers.
- Leads from different forms can go onto different lists.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the ActiveCampaign card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect ActiveCampaign?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in ActiveCampaign.
