Google Calendar is Google's calendar app, part of Gmail and Google Workspace.

With Claritia, your booking pages and forms offer only the times you're free on Google Calendar, and each new booking goes onto it with its Google Meet link and the guest invited.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Calendars |
| Connects with | Sign in with Google |
| Used in | **Meetings**, then **Availability**, then **Calendars** |
| Who can connect | Each person connects their own. Owners can disconnect anyone's. |
| Plan | Free: 1 calendar account per seat. Starter, Pro and Business: 6. |

## Connect Google Calendar

<Steps>

1. **Open Availability**

   In the sidebar, under **Meetings**, click **Availability**, then the **Calendars** tab.

   ![The Calendars tab with a Google account, Check for conflicts and Add new bookings to](../../../assets/shots/meetings/calendars.webp)

1. **Click Google Calendar**

   Under the accounts you have, click **Google Calendar**. You can also click it in the **Connect your calendar** line across the top of the Meetings pages, which shows until a calendar is connected.

1. **Sign in and allow access**

   Sign in with the account that holds your work calendar and approve Claritia. You come back to **Calendars**, and the account shows with its email.

1. **Pick the calendars that block times**

   Under **Check for conflicts**, each calendar in the account has a **Busy** switch. Turn it on for each calendar whose events should block your times.

1. **Pick where bookings go**

   Under **Add new bookings to**, pick the calendar that gets new meetings.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Google Calendar

- See your calendars and their events, to find when you're busy (`calendar.readonly`).
- Add, move and remove the events for meetings booked with you (`calendar.events`).
- See your email address, to show which account is connected (`userinfo.email`).

Claritia reads only when you're busy. It doesn't copy your event titles or details into Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Both ways. Google Calendar to Claritia for busy times. Claritia to Google Calendar for the events of meetings booked with you. |
| What's read | Busy times for the next 60 days, from each calendar with **Busy** on. |
| What's written | One event for each booking, in the calendar under **Add new bookings to**, with the guest invited and the Google Meet link when the meeting's location is Google Meet. Moving or canceling the meeting moves or removes the event. |
| When | Google tells Claritia when your calendar changes, and Claritia also reads it again every 10 minutes. Click **Read now** in the account's menu to read it at once. |

## Use Google Meet links

Pick **Google Meet** as a meeting type's **Location**. Each booking gets its own link, made with the calendar event, so there's no extra step. The link is in the invite, the confirmation email and the meeting's webhook.

## When it needs signing in again

If Claritia loses access, for example after a password change, the account shows **Reconnect**. The line at the top of the Meetings pages says **Reconnect Google Calendar** too. Until you reconnect, your times leave every booking page, so nobody books a time you can't see.

## Check your times

The top of **Availability** shows how many 30-minute times your booking page offers next week, like "Your page shows 24 open times next week". It turns amber under 5, or says what's missing.

## Disconnect

In **Calendars**, open the account's menu and click **Disconnect**. Confirm **Disconnect [email]?**. Claritia stops reading it, and its times stop blocking your page. Events already made stay on your calendar.

<Aside type="note">
Each person on the team connects their own calendar. Round robin and team meetings only offer a person's times once their calendar is connected.
</Aside>
