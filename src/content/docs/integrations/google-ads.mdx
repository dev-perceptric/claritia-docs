Google Ads runs ads on Google Search, YouTube and across millions of websites. You pay when people click, and Google tries to show ads to people likely to buy.

With Claritia, you can send conversions to Google Ads.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Analytics and ads |
| Connects with | Sign in with Google Ads |
| Used in | A form's **Settings**, then **Tracking** |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect Google Ads

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Google Ads" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Analytics and ads** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Google Ads's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Google Ads and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Google Ads

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Send conversions to Google Ads, like a form fill or a booked meeting.

## Send fills to Google Ads as offline conversions

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Settings**

   Open the form, click **Settings**, then **Tracking**.

1. **Pick the account and action**

   Under **Google Ads**, **Offline conversions** shows **Fills with a Google click ID** once connected. In **Account and action**, pick the **Google Ads account** and the **Conversion action**.

1. **Add the conversion tag too**

   Under **Google Ads conversion**, add the **Conversion ID and label** for the browser tag.

</Steps>

Only fills that came from a Google ad click carry a click ID, so only those are sent.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Google Ads. |
| What's sent | Fills that carry a Google click ID, as offline conversions. |
| When | Right after each fill or booking. |

## What teams use it for

- Tell Google which ads brought in real leads, so it bids smarter.
- Count bookings as conversions, not just clicks.
- See your true cost for each lead.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Google Ads card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Google Ads?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Google Ads.
