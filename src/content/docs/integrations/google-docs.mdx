Google Docs is Google's word processor. Teams write, edit and share documents in the browser, and several people can work on the same document at once.

With Claritia, you can make a Google Docs document for each form fill.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Documents and files |
| Connects with | Sign in with Google Docs |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect Google Docs

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Google Docs" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Documents and files** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Google Docs's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Google Docs and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Google Docs

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Read the template document you link.
- Create a new document from that template for every form fill.

## Make a document for each fill

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Google Docs** on the left.

1. **Turn on A document for each fill**

   Switch on **A document for each fill**.

1. **Link the template**

   Paste your template's link in **Template doc link** (it says **Paste the template doc's link**) and press Enter. Claritia shows **Template: [name]** and says **Each fill makes a doc from that template now**.

</Steps>

Answers drop into the template in the places you marked. To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop making documents in Google Docs?**.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Google Docs. Claritia reads the template you pick. |
| What's sent | A new document for each fill, made from your template, with the answers filled in. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every form fill creates a filled-in document, like a proposal or an intake summary.
- Answers drop into the right spots in your template, keeping your styling.
- Each person gets their own document, ready to share.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Google Docs card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Google Docs?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Google Docs.
