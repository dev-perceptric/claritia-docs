DocuSign is the most used tool for signing documents online. Businesses use it to send contracts, agreements and forms that people sign from any device.

With Claritia, you can make a DocuSign document for each form fill.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Documents and files |
| Connects with | Sign in with DocuSign |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect DocuSign

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "DocuSign" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Documents and files** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while DocuSign's sign-in page loads. Sign in to DocuSign and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in DocuSign

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your DocuSign templates.
- Send a document from a template to the person who filled your form.
- Fill the document with their answers before it's sent.

## Make a document for each fill

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **DocuSign** on the left.

1. **Turn on A document for each fill**

   Switch on **A document for each fill**.

1. **Pick the template**

   Choose from **Pick a template**. It reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads your DocuSign templates. Claritia says **Each fill gets [template] now**.

</Steps>

Answers drop into the template in the places you marked. To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop making documents in DocuSign?**.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to DocuSign. Claritia reads the template you pick. |
| What's sent | A new document for each fill, made from your template, with the answers filled in. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- After someone fills your form, they get a contract to sign right away.
- Their name and answers are already filled in.
- No more sending the same agreement by hand.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the DocuSign card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect DocuSign?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in DocuSign.
