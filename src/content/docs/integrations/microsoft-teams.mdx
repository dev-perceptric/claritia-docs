Microsoft Teams is Microsoft's chat and meeting app for work. Teams talk in channels, share files and hold video calls, all inside Microsoft 365.

With Claritia, you can post form alerts in Microsoft Teams.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Messages and texts |
| Connects with | Sign in with Microsoft Teams |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab and workflow steps |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Microsoft Teams

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Microsoft Teams" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Messages and texts** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Microsoft Teams's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Microsoft Teams and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Microsoft Teams

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your teams and channels.
- Post messages in the channel you pick.

## Post an alert for each fill

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Microsoft Teams** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Alert Microsoft Teams on each fill**, or switch on **Alert on each fill**.

1. **Pick the channel**

   Choose from **Pick a channel**. Claritia says **Alerts go to [channel] now**.

</Steps>

To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop alerts in Microsoft Teams?**.

### Post from a workflow

In a workflow, add the **Teams or Discord** step, in the **Outside** group. Pick Microsoft Teams and write the message. It starts as "A new meeting was just booked."

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Microsoft Teams. |
| What's sent | A message for each fill, and any message a workflow's **Teams or Discord** step sends. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Get a message in Teams every time someone fills a form.
- Your sales channel hears about hot leads right away.
- Workflows can post updates, like a meeting being booked.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Microsoft Teams card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Microsoft Teams?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Microsoft Teams.
