Google Sheets is Google's spreadsheet app. It's free, works in the browser, and many teams use it as a simple shared database.

With Claritia, you can add a row to Google Sheets for each form fill.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Sheets and databases |
| Connects with | Sign in with Google Sheets |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Google Sheets

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Google Sheets" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Sheets and databases** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Google Sheets's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Google Sheets and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Google Sheets

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See the spreadsheet you pick in Claritia.
- Add a new row for every form fill.
- Add a column when you add a new question to your form.

## Send a form's fills to Google Sheets

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Google Sheets** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send rows to Google Sheets**.

1. **Pick the sheet**

   Click **Start a new sheet** to make one named after the form, or paste a sheet's link under **Use a sheet I have** and click **Use**. Claritia says **Rows go to the new sheet now** or **Rows go to that sheet now**.

</Steps>

Each fill becomes one row. A new question adds a column.

To switch, use **Use another sheet** or pick again. To stop, click **Turn off** and confirm. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Google Sheets. |
| What's sent | One row for each fill, with every answer. |
| Repeats | A fill sent twice still makes only one. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every form fill becomes a new row, so your whole team can see answers in one sheet.
- Share the sheet with people who don't use Claritia.
- Use Sheets formulas and charts on your form answers.
- Fills that are sent twice still make only one row.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Google Sheets card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Google Sheets?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Google Sheets.
