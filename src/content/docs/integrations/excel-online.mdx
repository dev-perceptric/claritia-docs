Excel Online is Microsoft's spreadsheet app in the browser. It saves to OneDrive or SharePoint.

With Claritia, you can add a row to Excel Online for each form fill.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Sheets and databases |
| Connects with | Sign in with Excel Online |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Excel Online

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Excel Online" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Sheets and databases** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Excel Online's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Excel Online and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Excel Online

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See the workbook you pick in Claritia.
- Add a new row for every form fill.
- Add a column when you add a new question to your form.

## Send a form's fills to Excel Online

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Excel Online** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send rows to Excel Online**.

1. **Pick the workbook**

   Choose from **Pick a workbook**, or paste one's link under **Or paste its link** and click **Use**.

</Steps>

Each fill becomes one row.

To switch, use **Use another workbook** or pick again. To stop, click **Turn off** and confirm. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Excel Online. Claritia reads your workbooks so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | One row for each fill, with every answer. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every form fill becomes a new row in your Excel sheet.
- Teams on Microsoft 365 see answers without a new tool.
- Build reports and charts in Excel from your form answers.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Excel Online card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Excel Online?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Excel Online.
