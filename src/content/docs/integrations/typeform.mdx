Typeform is a form builder known for showing one question at a time. People use it for surveys, quizzes and lead forms that feel like a conversation.

With Claritia, you can bring Typeform answers into Claritia.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Forms |
| Connects with | Webhook address you paste in the tool |
| Used in | Settings › Integrations |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect Typeform

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Typeform" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Forms** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The Typeform sheet opens with one field, **Signing secret**. Type a long random secret, or the one you set in Typeform, and click **Get the webhook URL**.

1. **Copy the webhook address**

   The address is shown under the card with a **Copy** button. Later, click **Webhook URL** on the card to see it again.

1. **Paste it in Typeform**

   Add a webhook in Typeform with that address. See the steps below.

</Steps>

### Add the webhook in Typeform

The steps in Typeform's own screens may change. Check them against Typeform's help pages.

<Steps>

1. **Open the form in Typeform**

   Open the form, then **Connect**, then **Webhooks**. Click **Add a webhook** and paste Claritia's address.

1. **Set the secret**

   Click **Edit** on the webhook, set a **Secret**, and use the same text you typed in Claritia's **Signing secret**. Claritia checks Typeform's `typeform-signature` header with it.

1. **Turn it on**

   Switch the webhook on in Typeform. Click **Send test request**, or fill the form once, to check.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Typeform

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Claritia only receives the answers Typeform sends to it.
- Claritia can't see or change anything else in your Typeform account.

## What happens to each fill

Each fill in Typeform is sent to Claritia the moment it's made. Claritia matches the person by email, so a person who fills two forms stays one person. Answers come in by their question titles. Claritia never reads anything from Typeform on its own.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Typeform to Claritia, by webhook. |
| What's sent | Each fill's answers, by question title, and any hidden fields. |
| When | Within seconds of each fill or booking. Claritia never polls. |

## What teams use it for

- Keep using Typeform forms and still get every answer into Claritia.
- Send Typeform leads to your CRM, email lists and workflows through Claritia.
- Book meetings for people who fill your Typeform forms.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Typeform card and confirm **Disconnect Typeform?**. The webhook address stops working, so also remove the webhook in Typeform.
