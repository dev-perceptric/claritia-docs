Mixpanel is a product analytics tool. It shows what people do in your app or website, like which features they use and where they drop off.

With Claritia, you can send form fills to Mixpanel as events.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Analytics and ads |
| Connects with | A key from your Mixpanel account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab and workflow steps |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up |

## Connect Mixpanel

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Mixpanel" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Analytics and ads** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   Mixpanel has no sign-in for apps like Claritia, so the card's sheet opens a short form that asks for:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **Project token** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |
   | **Region (us or eu)** | No | `us` |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with Mixpanel. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what Mixpanel said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Mixpanel

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Send events to Mixpanel, like a form fill or a booked meeting.

## Send fills to Mixpanel

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Mixpanel** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send fills to Mixpanel**, or switch on **Form Submitted event on each fill**. Claritia says **Fills go to Mixpanel now**.

</Steps>

Each fill sends one **Form Submitted** event. To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop sending fills to Mixpanel?**.

### Send an event from a workflow

In a workflow, add the **Analytics event** step, in the **Outside** group. Under **Pick a tool**, choose Mixpanel, and type an **Event name**, like `Meeting Booked`. Until a tool is connected, the step says **Connect Segment, PostHog, Mixpanel or Amplitude first**.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Mixpanel. |
| What's sent | A **Form Submitted** event for each fill, and any event a workflow's **Analytics event** step sends. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- See form fills and bookings in Mixpanel next to everything else people do.
- Measure which pages and campaigns bring in leads.
- Workflows can send their own events, like a lead becoming qualified.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Mixpanel card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Mixpanel?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Mixpanel.
