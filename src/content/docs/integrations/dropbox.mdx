Dropbox is cloud storage for keeping and sharing files. People use it to back up files, send large files and work on shared folders with a team.

With Claritia, you can copy uploaded files to Dropbox.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Documents and files |
| Connects with | Sign in with Dropbox |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Dropbox

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Dropbox" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Documents and files** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Dropbox's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Dropbox and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Dropbox

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Create folders inside the folder you pick.
- Upload the files people send through your forms.
- Only work inside that folder.

## Copy uploaded files to Dropbox

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Dropbox** on the left. Your form needs a file upload question.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Copy files to Dropbox**, or switch on **Copy each fill's files**.

1. **Pick the folder**

   Choose from **Pick a folder**. Claritia says **Files go to [folder] now**.

</Steps>

Each fill's files are copied into that folder in Dropbox. To stop, click **Remove** and confirm **Stop copying files to Dropbox?**.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Dropbox. |
| What's sent | The files people upload in the form, copied into the folder you pick. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Files people upload in your forms are saved to Dropbox automatically.
- Each fill gets its own folder, named so you can find it.
- Resumes, photos and documents end up where your team already looks.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Dropbox card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Dropbox?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Dropbox.
