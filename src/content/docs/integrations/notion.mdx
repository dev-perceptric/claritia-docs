Notion is a workspace for notes, docs, wikis and databases. Teams use it to plan work and keep knowledge in one place.

With Claritia, you can add a row to Notion for each form fill.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Sheets and databases |
| Connects with | Sign in with Notion |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Notion

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Notion" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Sheets and databases** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while Notion's sign-in page loads. Sign in to Notion and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

If Notion's sign-in isn't set up on Claritia's side, Connect tells you and opens a short form for an **Integration secret** instead. You can also open the card and click **Use a key instead**. Keys are only a fallback.

## What Claritia can do in Notion

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See only the Notion pages and databases you share with Claritia.
- Add a new item to that database for every form fill.
- Add a property when you add a new question.

## Send a form's fills to Notion

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Notion** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send rows to Notion**.

1. **Pick the database**

   Choose from **Pick a database**. It reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads them from Notion. Claritia says **Rows go to [name] now**.

</Steps>

Each fill becomes one row.

To switch, use **Use another table** or pick again. To stop, click **Turn off** and confirm. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Notion. Claritia reads your databases so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | One row for each fill, with every answer. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every form fill becomes a new item in a Notion database.
- Collect feedback, applications or requests where your team already works.
- Link form answers to your other Notion pages.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Notion card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Notion?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Notion.
