Kit is an email marketing tool for creators and newsletter writers. It used to be called ConvertKit.

With Claritia, you can add form sign-ups to a Kit list.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Email lists |
| Connects with | A key from your Kit account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Kit

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Kit" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Email lists** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   Kit has no sign-in for apps like Claritia, so the card's sheet opens a short form that asks for:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **API secret** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with Kit. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what Kit said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Kit

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your Kit lists.
- Add new subscribers to the list you choose.
- Update subscribers who are already on that list.

## Add a form's sign-ups to a Kit form

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **Kit** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Add leads to Kit**.

1. **Pick the form**

   Choose from **Pick a form**. The menu reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads your forms from Kit. Claritia confirms with **Leads join [name] now**.

1. **Choose who subscribes**

   Set **Subscribe** to **Everyone** or **Who ticked consent**.

1. **Add tags**

   Under **Tags**, type a tag in **Add a tag** and press Enter. Each new person gets those tags in Kit. Click a tag's x to remove it.

</Steps>

To stop, click **Turn off** under **Stop sending to Kit** and confirm **Stop adding leads to Kit?**. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to Kit. Claritia reads your forms so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | The person's email and name, their consent, and your tags. |
| Match | By email. People already on the form are updated. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Form sign-ups become subscribers in Kit.
- Readers who sign up from different forms can be told apart.
- A returning subscriber is updated, not added twice.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Kit card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect Kit?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in Kit.
