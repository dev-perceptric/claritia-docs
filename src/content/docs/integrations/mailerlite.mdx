MailerLite is a simple email marketing tool. Small businesses and creators use it to send newsletters, build sign-up lists and set up automatic emails.

With Claritia, you can add form sign-ups to a MailerLite list.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Email lists |
| Connects with | A key from your MailerLite account |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect MailerLite

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "MailerLite" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Email lists** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   MailerLite connects with a key from your MailerLite account. Connect opens a secure connect page that asks for it. If that page isn't set up, the card's sheet opens a short form instead, with:

   | Field | Required | Notes |
   | - | - | - |
   | **API token** | Yes |  Hidden as you type. |

1. **Click Connect in the form**

   The button reads **Checking…** while Claritia tests the details with MailerLite. If they work, the card shows **Connected**. If not, the form shows what MailerLite said.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in MailerLite

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your MailerLite lists.
- Add new subscribers to the list you choose.
- Update subscribers who are already on that list.

## Add a form's sign-ups to a MailerLite group

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **MailerLite** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Add leads to MailerLite**.

1. **Pick the group**

   Choose from **Pick a group**. The menu reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads your groups from MailerLite. Claritia confirms with **Leads join [name] now**.

1. **Choose who subscribes**

   Set **Subscribe** to **Everyone** or **Who ticked consent**.

</Steps>

To stop, click **Turn off** under **Stop sending to MailerLite** and confirm **Stop adding leads to MailerLite?**. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to MailerLite. Claritia reads your groups so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | The person's email and name, and their consent. |
| Match | By email. People already on the group are updated. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Form sign-ups join your MailerLite group.
- A returning subscriber is updated, not added twice.
- New subscribers start your MailerLite welcome emails.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the MailerLite card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect MailerLite?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in MailerLite.
