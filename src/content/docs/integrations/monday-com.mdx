monday.com is a work platform where teams plan projects on colorful boards. Each row is an item, like a task or a request, that moves through stages.

With Claritia, you can make a monday.com item for each form fill.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Tasks and tickets |
| Connects with | Sign in with monday.com |
| Used in | A form's **Sync** tab |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect monday.com

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "monday.com" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Tasks and tickets** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The button reads **Opening…** while monday.com's sign-in page loads. Sign in to monday.com and approve Claritia.

1. **Come back to Claritia**

   You land back on **Integrations**. The card shows **Connected**, and the account you signed in with when there is one.

</Steps>

If monday.com's sign-in isn't set up on Claritia's side, Connect tells you and opens a short form for an **API token** instead. You can also open the card and click **Use a key instead**. Keys are only a fallback.

## What Claritia can do in monday.com

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- See your monday.com boards.
- Create a new item for every form fill.
- Find items it made before, so it never makes the same one twice.

## Make a monday.com item for each fill

<Steps>

1. **Open the form's Sync tab**

   Open the form, click **Sync** and pick **monday.com** on the left.

1. **Turn it on**

   Click **Send rows to monday.com**.

1. **Pick the board**

   Choose from **Pick a board**. It reads **Reading…** while Claritia loads them from monday.com. Claritia says **Fills go to [name] now**.

</Steps>

Each fill becomes one item in that board, with every answer inside.

To switch, use **Use another table** or pick again. To stop, click **Turn off** and confirm. Fills stay in Claritia.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Claritia to monday.com. Claritia reads your boards so you can pick one. |
| What's sent | One item for each fill, with every answer. |
| Repeats | A fill sent twice still makes only one. |
| When | Right after each fill. If the tool is busy or down, Claritia tries again after about 1, 5 and 30 minutes, then 2 and 6 hours. |
| While off | Fills are kept. When you turn it on again, what it held is sent, oldest first. |

## What teams use it for

- Every form fill becomes an item on your monday.com board.
- Project requests land in the right board with all the details.
- Your team tracks each request through to done.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the monday.com card, or open the card and click it there. Confirm **Disconnect monday.com?**. Forms stop sending to it. What was already sent stays in monday.com.
