Tally is a simple, free form builder. You make a form the way you write a document, and it's popular with startups and creators.

With Claritia, you can bring Tally answers into Claritia.

| | |
| - | - |
| Category | Forms |
| Connects with | Webhook address you paste in the tool |
| Used in | Settings › Integrations |
| Who can connect | Workspace owners |
| Plan | Starter and up (listed on the pricing page; check) |

## Connect Tally

Only workspace owners can connect or disconnect tools. Other members see the card and its state.

<Steps>

1. **Open Integrations**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Integrations**. Type "Tally" in **Search integrations**, or pick **Forms** in the category menu.

1. **Click Connect**

   The Tally sheet opens with one field, **Signing secret**. Type a long random secret, or the one you set in Tally, and click **Get the webhook URL**.

1. **Copy the webhook address**

   The address is shown under the card with a **Copy** button. Later, click **Webhook URL** on the card to see it again.

1. **Paste it in Tally**

   Add a webhook in Tally with that address. See the steps below.

</Steps>

### Add the webhook in Tally

The steps in Tally's own screens may change. Check them against Tally's help pages.

<Steps>

1. **Open the form in Tally**

   Open the form, then **Integrations**, then **Webhooks**. Click **Connect** and paste Claritia's address.

1. **Set the signing secret**

   Turn on **Signing secret** and use the same text you typed in Claritia.

1. **Test it**

   Fill the form once. The fill comes into Claritia within seconds.

</Steps>

## What Claritia can do in Tally

When you connect, Claritia asks for this access:

- Claritia only receives the answers Tally sends to it.
- Claritia can't see or change anything else in your Tally account.

## What happens to each fill

Each fill in Tally is sent to Claritia the moment it's made. Claritia matches the person by email, so a person who fills two forms stays one person. Answers come in by their question titles. Claritia never reads anything from Tally on its own.

## What syncs

| | |
| - | - |
| Direction | Tally to Claritia, by webhook. |
| What's sent | Each fill's answers, by question title, and any hidden fields. |
| When | Within seconds of each fill or booking. Claritia never polls. |

## What teams use it for

- Keep using Tally forms and still get every answer into Claritia.
- Send Tally leads to your CRM, email lists and workflows through Claritia.
- Book meetings for people who fill your Tally forms.

## Disconnect

In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on the Tally card and confirm **Disconnect Tally?**. The webhook address stops working, so also remove the webhook in Tally.
