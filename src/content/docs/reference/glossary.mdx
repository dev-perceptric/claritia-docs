Words you'll see in Claritia, in plain terms.

**Account owner first**
: A meeting kind where a lead who already has an owner in your CRM books with that owner. Everyone else goes to the team. On Business.

**AI credit**
: One unit of work Tia does for you. A small change uses 1 credit. A whole new form uses about 10. Each plan includes a number each month.

**Always on**
: A meeting workflow that carries the calendar invite: Booking confirmed, Meeting moved and Meeting canceled. It can't be paused or deleted.

**Audience**
: The people your emails can go to, in **Email**, then **Audiences**. Lists group them.

**Booking page**
: A page that lists a person's or a team's meetings, with a calendar to book a time. Also an **Ending type** that puts a calendar at the end of a form.

**Collective**
: A team meeting where everyone on the team attends. Guests only see times when all of them are free.

**Days off**
: Whole days you're away. They leave every booking page, and team meetings skip you.

**Draft**
: A form or workflow that isn't live yet, or the changes you've made since you last published.

**Dynamic meeting**
: A meeting type with rules that pick the host from the guest's answers, and **Otherwise** for everyone else.

**Ending**
: The last screen of a form: a thank-you page, a redirect to your site, or a booking page.

**Form check**
: The check that runs when you click **Publish**. It stops on real problems, like a rule that reads a deleted question.

**Form Studio**
: Claritia's form editor, where you build forms by clicking or by chatting with Tia.

**Individual**
: A meeting type with one host. Everyone who books it books with that person.

**Left early**
: People who started a form and didn't send it. Claritia saves the question they were on, never their answers. On Business.

**Link domain**
: Your own web address for forms and booking pages, like `forms.acme.com`. On Business.

**Live**
: Published and taking fills or running for real.

**Meeting type**
: Something people can book, with its length, location, host, hours and limits.

**Meeting workflows**
: The workflows in the **Meetings** folder that send the emails every meeting needs, like the confirmation and reminders.

**No-show**
: A meeting the guest didn't come to. Marking one can send the guest a link to book again.

**On submission**
: What happens after someone sends a form, set in the form's Ending: which meeting they book and what every send does.

**Otherwise**
: The last rule in a set. It catches everyone the rules above don't.

**Owner**
: In a workspace, the role that can change billing, members and domains. For a lead, the teammate who looks after them.

**Poll**
: A link that offers a group several times to vote on. You then book the winning time.

**Round robin**
: A way for a team to share meetings by taking turns. People who are away or at their cap are skipped.

**Seat**
: A teammate people can book a meeting with. You pay per seat. Teammates who only build or read are free.

**Single-use link**
: A link to a meeting type that books once, then closes.

**Spam**
: Fills that a spam check caught. They wait in the form's Spam list, where **Not spam** lets one in.

**Submission**
: One sent form, with its answers. Also called a response or a fill.

**Team**
: A group of people who share meetings, set up in **Availability**, under **Team**.

**Tia**
: Claritia's AI assistant. Tia builds and changes forms, and writes emails in your brand's voice.

**Trigger**
: What starts a workflow, like a form fill or a booked meeting.

**Workflow**
: A set of steps that runs on its own after a trigger, like sending an email, waiting or posting to Slack.

**Workspace**
: Your team's space in Claritia, with its own forms, meetings, workflows, members and plan.
