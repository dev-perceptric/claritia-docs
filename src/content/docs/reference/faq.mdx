## Getting started

<details>
<summary>Do I need a card to start?</summary>

No. Free is for one seat and needs no card. Sign in with Google, Microsoft or an email link and start building. See [Sign up and set up](/get-started/sign-up/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does it take to set up?</summary>

Most teams have a live form that books meetings in about 15 minutes: connect a calendar, make a meeting type, pick a form and put it on your site. See [Your first week](/get-started/first-week/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I bring my forms from another tool?</summary>

Yes. On the **Forms** page, click **Import a form** and paste a Google Forms, Typeform or Tally link. If you'd rather keep Typeform, Tally or Webflow forms, connect them in **Settings**, then **Integrations**, and their fills come into Claritia.

</details>

## Plans and seats

<details>
<summary>What is a seat?</summary>

A seat is a teammate people can book a meeting with. Teammates who only build forms, edit workflows or read results are free, so you only pay for the people who take calls.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens after 250 submissions on Free?</summary>

Your forms keep working and every submission is saved. Submissions past 250 show once you upgrade, or on the 1st of the next month. No lead is lost.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is an AI credit?</summary>

One thing Tia does for you, like writing a form or adding questions. A small change is 1 credit and a whole new form is about 10. When credits run out, Tia waits for the next month. Your forms, meetings and reminders keep running.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if a paid plan passes its submissions?</summary>

Every submission is saved and shows right away. Extra submissions are $5 per 1,000 on Starter and Pro. Business has no limit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I change or cancel my plan?</summary>

Yes, any time, from **Settings**, then **Billing**. An upgrade starts right away. A cancel takes effect at the end of the period you paid for. See [Plans and billing](/workspace/plans-and-billing/).

</details>

## Forms

<details>
<summary>Can people book a meeting right inside my form?</summary>

Yes. In Form Studio, open the **Ending**, set **Ending type** to **Booking page** and pick the meeting. The calendar opens as soon as they send the form. See [On submission](/forms/on-submission/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why don't my changes show on the live form?</summary>

Changes save to a draft. Click **Publish changes** to make them live. The form's tag says **Changes not live** while your draft differs.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I stop a form for a while?</summary>

Open the form's menu on the **Forms** page and pause it, or use **Pause the form** in **Settings**, then **Access**. People see your closed message until you resume it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I remove the Claritia badge?</summary>

The badge is smaller on Starter and gone on Pro and Business.

</details>

## Meetings

<details>
<summary>Which calendars work with Claritia?</summary>

Google Calendar and Outlook, including Microsoft 365. Meetings can get a Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Webex or Whereby link, or take place by phone or in person.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can two people book the same time?</summary>

No. When a guest picks a time, Claritia holds it for them for 5 minutes, and it checks the host's calendar again before it books. If a time was just taken, the guest sees fresh times.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I share leads across my team?</summary>

Make a team in **Availability**, under **Team**, and use it in a round robin or a Dynamic meeting, or in a form's Team meeting rules. See [Team meetings](/meetings/team-meetings/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do guests need an account to reschedule?</summary>

No. The confirmation email and the booked screen have links to reschedule or cancel that need no sign-in.

</details>

## Emails and workflows

<details>
<summary>Where do I change the reminder emails?</summary>

In **Email**, then **Workflows**, open the **Meetings** folder and the **Reminders** workflow. Change the words or the times, then publish. See [Meeting emails and reminders](/email/meeting-emails/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does anything send before I publish a workflow?</summary>

No. New workflows and templates are drafts. Nothing sends until you click **Publish**. The meeting workflows are the exception: they're live from the start, so every meeting gets its confirmation and reminders.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens when I reach my monthly workflow emails?</summary>

Emails past the limit wait until the next month or until you upgrade. They're never dropped.

</details>

## Integrations and developers

<details>
<summary>Can Claritia send leads to my CRM?</summary>

Yes. Connect it in **Settings**, then **Integrations**, then turn it on in the form's **Sync** tab. HubSpot, Omniscient, Pipedrive, Attio and the other CRMs are on Starter and up. Salesforce is on Business.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is there an API?</summary>

Yes. Make a key in **Settings**, then **API keys**. You can read forms and events, subscribe hooks and add people to workflows. See [API and webhooks](/developers/api/). AI apps can connect through the MCP server. See [Connect your AI app](/developers/mcp/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I get help?</summary>

Write to [hello@tryclaritia.com](mailto:hello@tryclaritia.com). Every plan includes email help.

</details>
