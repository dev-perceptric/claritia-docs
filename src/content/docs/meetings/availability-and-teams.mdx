**Availability** holds everyone's hours, calendars and time away. Forms and booking pages both read it, so a lead only sees times when the host is free. Open it from the sidebar, under **Meetings**. The switch at the top picks **You** or **Team**.

## Your hours

With **You** picked, the page has three columns side by side.

| Column | What it holds |
| - | - |
| **Schedules** | Your **Working hours**, which are the default, and any named schedules a meeting type can use |
| **The week** | Each day as a row, with a switch to turn it on, its times, and a way to add more times |
| **Calendars** | Your connected accounts, which ones are checked for conflicts, and where new bookings go |

Changes to a week save on their own a moment after you stop typing.

### Named schedules

Click **New schedule** to make a second set of hours, like "Demo hours". Then pick it in a meeting type's **Availability** section. If you delete a schedule, the meeting types that used it go back to your working hours.

### Date-specific hours and days off

- **Date-specific hours** change your times on one date, such as a late start.
- **Days off** block whole days. Those days leave every booking page, and team meetings skip you.

<Aside type="tip">
Working hours start as Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. Check them, and your time zone, before you share your first meeting.
</Aside>

## The team view

Pick **Team** to see everyone in one table:

| Column | What it shows |
| - | - |
| **Person** | Each teammate |
| **Teams** | The teams they're in |
| **Calendar** | Whether their calendar is connected, or needs **Reconnect** |
| **Hours** | Their working hours |
| **Today** | Meetings booked today, against their daily cap |
| **Away** | Whether they're away, and until when |

### Mark someone away

Turn on **Away** for a person and pick the day they're back. While they're away, team meetings pass them over. Turn it off to bring them back today.

### Daily caps

Set a cap on the meetings a person takes each day. Once they reach it, the team's turns skip them until tomorrow. A person's cap and away status are the same in every team.

## Teams

A team is a group of people who share meetings, like "Sales, North America". Teams power round robin and Collective meetings.

<Steps>

1. **Pick a team**

   Use the team menu above the table. The table filters to its members first, then everyone else with **Add**.

1. **Add people**

   Click **Add** next to a person to put them on the team.

1. **Set how the team shares**

   Under **How the team shares**, pick round robin, weighted round robin or load balanced. The table shows who is **Next in line**.

1. **Check what uses it**

   **Used by** lists the meetings and forms that send leads to the team.

</Steps>

You can also make a team right from a form's rules with **New team**. See [On submission](/forms/on-submission/).

You can't delete a team while a meeting or form still sends to it. Change those first.

<Aside type="note">
Round robin with weights, away days and daily and weekly limits for each person are on Pro and Business.
</Aside>
