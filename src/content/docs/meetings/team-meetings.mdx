A team meeting lets more than one person take bookings. Claritia picks who hosts each one, fairly, and keeps a note of why.

## Individual or Dynamic

Each meeting type's **Host** section has two choices:

| Host | How it works |
| - | - |
| **Individual** | One host. Everyone who books this meeting books with that person. |
| **Dynamic** | A group of people and rules. Each rule picks one person or a team that takes turns. **Otherwise** catches everyone else. |

## Set up a Dynamic meeting

<Steps>

1. **Open the meeting type**

   Go to **Meeting types**, click the card, and open the **Host** section. Pick **Dynamic**.

1. **Add a rule**

   A rule is one or more conditions on the guest's answers, like "Company size is at least 50". Then pick who gets those guests: one person, or a team.

1. **Set Otherwise**

   Under **Otherwise**, pick who gets everyone the rules don't catch.

1. **Save changes**

   Every live form that books this meeting picks up the new rules at once.

</Steps>

When someone opens a Dynamic meeting's own booking link, Claritia first asks the questions its rules test. Then it shows the times of the person or team their answers pick. A link that already names the host skips the questions.

<Aside type="note">
When a form books a Dynamic meeting, the form's answers feed the same rules, so the guest isn't asked twice. See [On submission](/forms/on-submission/).
</Aside>

## How a team takes turns

Teams live in **Availability**, under **Team**. Each team shares meetings in one of three ways:

| Way | Who gets the next meeting |
| - | - |
| **Round robin** | The person who is most due, so everyone gets about the same number |
| **Weighted round robin** | Like round robin, but people with a bigger weight get more |
| **Load balanced** | The person with the fewest meetings this week |

Whatever the way, people who are away, outside their hours or at their daily cap are skipped. If a lead already has an owner, they book with that owner first.

## Collective meetings

A **Collective** meeting needs everyone on the team. The guest only sees times when every member is free, and every member is in the meeting. In a form's rules, pick a team with **Collective**. In a meeting type's **All settings**, pick the **Collective** kind.

## More kinds in All settings

The full editor of a meeting type (its menu, then **All settings**) has more ways to share:

- **Round robin**: set each person's weight from 1 to 10, and a priority for ties. A **flexible** round robin shows every free host's times. A **strict** one only shows the next host's times.
- **Account owner first**: a guest who already has an owner in your CRM books with them. Everyone else goes to the team.
- **Paired round robin**: one person from each of two teams joins the meeting.
- **Reschedule**: choose whether a moved meeting keeps the same host or can go to anyone in the pool.

## See why someone got a lead

Open a meeting from **Upcoming**. **Why this person** shows the rule that matched and why that person was picked. See [Upcoming meetings](/meetings/upcoming/).
