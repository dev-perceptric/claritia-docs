In Claritia, the form picks the meeting and the meeting picks the person. You set the first part here, in the form's **Ending**. The second part lives on the meeting type. See [Team meetings](/meetings/team-meetings/).

## Pick the ending

In Form Studio, click **Ending** at the bottom of the **Pages** list. The panel on the right opens with **Ending type**:

| Ending type | What happens |
| - | - |
| **Thank-you page** | The thank-you message takes the form's place |
| **Redirect** | People go to the page address you type, like `https://yourcompany.com/thanks` |
| **Booking page** | A calendar shows under the thank-you message, so they can book on the spot |

The thank-you page and booking page can drop a little confetti when they show. Turn on **Show confetti** to use it.

## Book a meeting from the form

<Steps>

1. **Make a meeting type first**

   If you have none, the Ending shows **No meeting types yet** with **Open Meetings**. Make one there, then come back. See [Meeting types](/meetings/meeting-types/).

1. **Set the Ending type to Booking page**

   Under **Book with**, pick **Individual** or **Team meeting**.

1. **Pick the meeting**

   With **Individual**, everyone books the one meeting you pick. If that meeting is Dynamic, its rules show in one line, and **Edit meeting** opens it.

1. **Publish**

   Click **Publish changes**. The calendar now shows at the end of the live form, with the person's name and email already filled in.

</Steps>

## Route by answers with a Team meeting

Pick **Team meeting** to send different leads to different meetings or people. You add named rules, and they run in order. The first rule that matches wins. **Otherwise** sits last and catches everyone else.

Each rule reads like a sentence:

- **When** a question, a test and a value, such as "Team size is at least 50". Add more with **+ and** (all must match) or **+ or** (any can match).
- **Book** the meeting to book.
- **With** who they book with:

| With | Who hosts |
| - | - |
| **Meeting hosts** | The meeting's own host, or its own Dynamic rules |
| A team, with its distribution | One person on the team, chosen in turn, such as "Sales (Round robin)" |
| A team, **Collective** | Everyone on the team attends, at a time when all are free |
| One person | That teammate |
| **New team** | Makes a new team, named in place, that you fill with people |

When you pick a team, it opens under the rule. **First** is always **Account owner**, so a lead who already has an owner books with them. Then set the **Distribution** (Round robin, Weighted round robin or Load balanced) and the team's members. Changes save to the team itself, so every form that uses it follows.

Move rules up or down to change their order, and delete the ones you don't need.

### Rules on past fills

Besides answers, a rule can test what a person did before:

- **Has sent this form before**
- **Has a meeting booked**
- **Booked in the last N days**

Spam and test sends never count as past fills.

### Preview as a lead

With a Team meeting, **Preview as** shows a chip for each rule and **Otherwise** over the canvas. Click a chip to run a sample lead that meets that rule through every rule, just like a real lead. The booking page then shows the meeting that lead would really get. If an earlier rule catches them first, a line says so.

<Aside type="caution">
If an earlier rule always catches a rule's leads, that rule can never run. Its card says so, and the form check shows a warning.
</Aside>

## If no one is free

When a Team meeting or a Dynamic meeting finds no open times, pick what the lead sees:

- **Show the thank-you**: the thank-you message, with no calendar.
- **Email times later**: Claritia emails the lead open times a day later.

## What every send does

The **Notification** section runs on every send, whatever it books:

| Item | What it does |
| - | - |
| **Email the team** | Emails your team about each new submission |
| **Post to Slack** | Posts each new submission in your team's Slack channel |
| **Send to a tool** | Sends the lead to a connected CRM or a webhook. See [Send leads to your tools](/integrations/send-leads/) |
| **Follow-up** | Starts a workflow on this form, either when they send it or when they don't book |
| **Add to a list** | Adds each person to a list in **Email**, then **Audiences** |

**Email the team** and **Post to Slack** are the same team alert you'll find in the form's **Settings**. Changing it in one place changes it in both.

<Aside type="note">
Routing by answers to a meeting and a person is on Pro and Business. Account owner first and pairing one person from each of two teams are on Business. See [Plans and billing](/workspace/plans-and-billing/).
</Aside>
