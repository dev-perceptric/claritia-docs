Claritia has 840 form templates for 38 industries. Each one comes in one of 18 styles, and you can switch it to your own brand before you use it. Templates are free on every plan.

## Open the templates

Go to **Forms** and click **Form templates**. With nothing picked, the page shows starter kits and forms that fit the season. Below them is the full grid.

## Filter the grid

The menus on the side narrow the grid. Two picks in the same menu widen the results. Picks in two different menus narrow them. Each count shows how many forms that pick would show.

| Menu | What it sorts by |
| - | - |
| **Category** | 85 categories in 10 groups, from Leads and sales to Consent and legal |
| **Industry** | 38 industries and their sub-industries |
| **Team** | The team that uses the form, like sales or HR |
| **What it does next** | Books a meeting, takes payment, shows a price, shows a score, sends a follow-up, or stays anonymous |
| **Features** | Logic, calculator, payment, booking, score, file upload, signature and more |
| **Layout** | One page, steps, or one question at a time |
| **Length** | Under 1 minute, 1 to 3 minutes, or over 3 minutes |

You can also search by words. Your picks stay in the page address, so you can share a filtered view with a teammate.

## Use a template

<Steps>

1. **Open a card**

   Click a template to see the live form. The panel shows what it comes with: the form, and for some templates a meeting and a follow-up.

1. **Pick the look**

   Keep the template's own style, pick one of your workspace's brands, or pick **Plain**.

1. **Click Use form template**

   Claritia makes the form and opens it in Form Studio. Change anything you like, then publish.

</Steps>

### Templates that book or follow up

- A **booking template** books your workspace's meeting type when you have exactly one. If you have more, pick the meeting yourself in the form's Ending.
- A template with a **follow-up** adds a draft workflow for the new form. Its emails send nothing until you publish the workflow.

### Starter kits

A starter kit makes 3 to 5 forms for one industry in one click, each in its own style. If one form fails to make, the rest still go through.

## Describe the form you need

At the end of the grid, type what you need in **Describe the form you need**. Claritia shows the closest templates first. Click **Build with AI** to open Form Studio with your words ready in Tia's box. Nothing is sent until you send it.

## Import a form you already have

<Steps>

1. **Copy the form's link**

   Copy the public link of a Google Forms, Typeform or Tally form.

1. **Import it**

   On the **Forms** page, click **Import a form**, paste the link and click **Import**.

1. **Check the notes**

   Claritia tells you how many questions it brought over, with a note for anything it couldn't copy.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
**Import a form** shows once you have at least one form. To import as your very first form, make one from a template first, then import.
</Aside>
