Everyone on your team can work in the same Claritia workspace. You only pay for the people leads can book a meeting with.

## Invite a teammate

<Steps>

1. **Open Members**

   Go to **Settings**, then **Members**.

1. **Click Invite**

   Type your teammate's email, like `name@company.com`, and click **Invite**.

1. **They sign in**

   Your teammate gets an email with a link. They sign in with that same email and land in your workspace.

</Steps>

Invited people show as **Invited** until they join. To cancel an invite, click the bin next to it. Inviting the same email again sends the email again.

You can also invite people during the guided setup, or type several emails with commas between them.

## Roles

Claritia has two roles.

| Role | What they can do |
| - | - |
| **Owner** | Everything, including inviting and removing people, renaming the workspace, changing the plan and billing, adding email domains and your own link domain, and managing meeting webhooks |
| **Member** | Build forms, meetings and workflows, read answers, and connect their own calendar |

The person who made the workspace is its owner. To remove a member, click the bin next to their name and confirm.

<Aside type="note">
Some actions made with an API key also need an owner's key, like publishing a form or scheduling a send to a list. See [API and webhooks](/developers/api/).
</Aside>

## Seats

A seat is a teammate people can book a meeting with. Teammates who only build forms, edit workflows or read results are free. So you only pay for the people who take calls.

Claritia counts seats from your active meeting types: everyone who hosts at least one. If you turn off a meeting type or take someone off its hosts, they stop counting.

| Plan | Seats |
| - | - |
| Free | 1 |
| Starter, Pro and Business | As many as you need, paid per seat |

Your current number of seats shows in **Settings**, then **Billing**, under **This month**.

## Several workspaces

If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them from the workspace name at the top of the sidebar. Claritia remembers your pick on that computer.

To rename your workspace, go to **Settings**, then **General**, and click **Rename**. Only owners can rename it.
