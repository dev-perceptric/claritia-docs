This page explains how Claritia keeps your account and your leads' data safe, and what you control.

## Signing in

- You sign in with Google, Microsoft or a link sent to your email. There's no password to forget or leak.
- An email sign-in link only works in the browser that asked for it. If you open it somewhere else, type the 6-digit code from the same email instead.
- Claritia limits how many sign-in emails one address or network can ask for in an hour.
- A bot check runs in the background on the sign-in screen. Most people never see it.
- The sign-in screen tags the way you signed in last on that computer as **Last used**.

To sign out, go to **Settings**, then **Profile**, and click **Sign out**.

## Connected accounts and keys

- Calendar and tool connections are stored sealed. Each tool's card in **Integrations** shows the access it gives.
- You can disconnect any calendar in **Availability** and any tool in **Settings**, then **Integrations**.
- API keys are stored as a hash. A new key shows once, so copy it then. Revoke a key in **Settings**, then **API keys**, and anything using it stops at once.
- Payments go straight to your own Stripe, PayPal or Square account. Claritia never holds the money.

## Your forms

- Spam checks run on every fill: a bot check, a hidden box only bots fill, a speed check, an hourly cap, throwaway email blocking and a list of sites allowed to show the form. See [Form settings](/forms/form-settings/#spam-and-security).
- The consent box can show for EU and UK visitors, for everyone, or for no one. Leads who don't agree never get marketing emails.
- People who leave a form early never have their answers saved. Claritia only records the question they were on.
- Webhooks only go to public `https://` addresses and are signed, so your system can check they came from Claritia.

## Your emails

- Every email is checked as it leaves for consent, unsubscribes and bounces.
- Each marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and a footer with your address is added when an email has none.
- Spam complaints stop sending to that address at once. Sending pauses if complaints pass 0.2%.

## Keep, export and delete

| What | Where |
| - | - |
| How long a form keeps answers | The form's **Settings**, then **Data**, then **Keep submissions** |
| Export a form's answers | **Export CSV** on **Submissions**, or **Export every submission** in **Data** |
| Delete one answer | Open it in **Submissions** and click **Delete** |
| Delete all of a form's answers | **Delete every submission** in **Data** |

## Your own emails from Claritia

**Settings**, then **Profile**, has your sign-in and the **Getting-started tips** switch. Turn it off to stop the short tip emails new workspaces get.
