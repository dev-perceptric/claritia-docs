Claritia reads your calendar to find when you're busy, then offers only the free times. Each new booking goes onto your calendar with its video link. Each person on your team connects their own calendar.

## Connect a calendar

<Steps>

1. **Open Availability**

   In the sidebar, under **Meetings**, click **Availability**. Make sure **You** is picked at the top.

1. **Pick your calendar app**

   In the **Calendars** column, click **Google Calendar** or **Outlook**. Outlook covers Microsoft 365 too.

1. **Sign in and allow access**

   Sign in with the account that holds your work calendar and approve Claritia. You come back to Availability, and the account shows as connected.

1. **Choose your calendars**

   Turn on **Check for conflicts** for each calendar whose events should block times. Under **Add new bookings to**, pick the calendar where new meetings go.

</Steps>

Until a calendar is connected, a slim line across the top of the Meetings pages offers **Google Calendar** and **Outlook**, so you can connect from there too.

## Add Zoom

To give meetings a Zoom link, click **Zoom** in the same **Calendars** column and sign in. Then pick Zoom as the **Location** of a meeting type. Each booking gets its own Zoom link on your Zoom account. Google Meet and Microsoft Teams links come with your Google or Microsoft calendar, so they need no extra step.

## How many calendars you can connect

| Plan | Calendar accounts per seat |
| - | - |
| Free | 1 |
| Starter, Pro and Business | 6 |

## Keep it working

- Claritia keeps your busy times fresh on its own. Click **Read now** in an account's menu to read it again right away.
- If Claritia loses access, for example after a password change, the account shows **Reconnect**. You also get one email asking you to reconnect. Until you do, your times leave every booking page.
- To stop using an account, open its menu and click **Disconnect**.

<Aside type="note">
Your working hours live on the same page. Set them before you share a meeting. See [Availability and teams](/meetings/availability-and-teams/).
</Aside>
